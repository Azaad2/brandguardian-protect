# BndBox - Wholesale Made Easy, Brands Meet Reseller/Retailers

## About BndBox
BndBox is a comprehensive marketplace platform that connects premium brands with verified resellers and retailers. We specialize in Amazon wholesale approval, brand protection, MAP policy enforcement, and authorized distribution across major marketplaces including Amazon, Walmart, and eBay.

## Core Services
- Brand wholesale approval processes
- Authorized reseller verification and onboarding
- MAP (Minimum Advertised Price) policy enforcement
- Brand protection and compliance monitoring
- Marketplace distribution management
- Amazon brand registry assistance

## Target Audience
- Premium brands seeking authorized resellers
- Resellers and retailers looking for wholesale opportunities
- Amazon FBA sellers seeking brand approvals
- E-commerce businesses expanding their product catalog
